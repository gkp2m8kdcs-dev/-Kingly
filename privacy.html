1. Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to Kingly (the "App"), an iOS application published under the name "Kingly" and distributed via the Apple App Store. This Policy describes how the App handles information when you install and use it on your device.

By installing or using the App, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

2. Data We Collect

Kingly is designed as a fully on-device wealth journal. We collect nothing. Specifically, the App does not collect, transmit to any server, or share with any third party:

  • Personal identifying information (name, email, phone, address, SSN, etc.)
  • Financial account credentials, routing numbers, or card numbers
  • Transaction history, balances, holdings, budgets, or net worth data
  • Receipt images or document scans (beyond what is stored locally in the app sandbox)
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs (IDFA), or advertising profiles
  • Location data, contacts, calendars, or any other device data
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, telemetry, or diagnostics

All information you enter into the App — including accounts, transactions, categories, holdings, budgets, and net worth snapshots — is stored exclusively on your device inside the iOS app sandbox. The developer has no remote access to this data.

3. What We Do NOT Collect

To be explicit: the App contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, no tracking pixels, no remote configuration, no A/B testing framework, and no remote logging. There is no code in the App that sends your data anywhere.

We do not sell, rent, lease, or trade any user information because we have none.

4. Device Permissions

The App requests only the minimum device permissions required to deliver its features. All permissions are used solely to operate features on your device and never to transmit data.

Camera

Used only for capturing receipts to attach to transactions you create. Captured images are stored in the app sandbox and are never uploaded.

Photo Library (Read)

Used only when you choose to attach an existing photo from your library to a transaction. The App reads only the image you select and stores it locally; it does not enumerate or upload your photo library.

Photo Library (Write)

Used only when you choose to save an exported chart image or report to your Photos library. No other photos are written.

5. Local Storage & Security

All data is stored inside the iOS app sandbox, which is protected by iOS sandboxing and the iOS Data Protection class configured by the App. The App does not expose any data via network ports and does not run any background networking.

Because your data lives only on your device, the security of that data depends in part on the security of your device. We recommend:

  • Using a device passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID.
  • Keeping iOS up to date.
  • Enabling "Find My iPhone" so you can remotely erase the device if it is lost or stolen.
  • Exporting your data periodically as a personal backup.

Deleting your data: Deleting the App from your device permanently removes all stored data. You can also reset the App from Settings → Data → Delete All Data, which requires you to type the word "DELETE" to confirm. We recommend exporting a JSON snapshot first.

6. Third-Party Services

Kingly does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, social, or backend services. Apple's iOS, UIKit, Core Graphics, and standard system frameworks are used to provide on-device functionality, and Apple's standard App Store distribution mechanisms apply when you download or update the App.

Because we do not transmit data to any third party, this Policy does not describe third-party data practices. Apple's privacy practices are governed by Apple's own Privacy Policy.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, because we do not collect personal information from anyone. If you are a parent or guardian and have questions, please contact us using the address below.

8. International Users

The App is distributed globally via the Apple App Store. Because no personal data is transmitted from the App to the developer or any third party, there is no international data transfer to describe. Each user keeps their data on their device in their own country.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Because the App does not collect contact information, we cannot notify you directly of changes; we encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.

If a future update changes a material practice — for example, the introduction of an optional cloud sync feature — we will clearly disclose the change and obtain your consent before it takes effect.
