Frequently Asked Questions

How is my net worth calculated?

Kingly sums the current balance of every active account (cash, checking, savings, investment) and subtracts any liabilities you've recorded. Snapshots are taken automatically each time you log a transaction, so the trend line updates without any extra steps.

How do I update prices for my investments?

Open any holding, tap the price field, and enter the latest value. Cost basis, quantity, and current price combine to show your unrealized gain/loss in real time. Kingly does not connect to a live data feed — you remain in control of the numbers you record.

Can I use multiple currencies?

Kingly supports one display currency at a time, set in Settings → Preferences → Currency. All amounts are entered and aggregated in that single currency.

How are asset allocation percentages computed?

The allocation donut divides the current value of every holding across your asset classes (equities, bonds, crypto, real estate, cash, alternatives). Tap any slice to drill into the individual positions that contribute to it.

Does the App sync between my iPhone and iPad?

No. Kingly is local to each device, by design. If you want the same data on multiple devices, use the JSON export/import workflow.

I lost my data after reinstalling the App. Can you restore it?

Because data lives only on your device, we cannot restore anything we never had. We strongly recommend exporting a JSON snapshot periodically and keeping it in your Files app or iCloud Drive as a personal backup.

The App is crashing. What should I do?

First, make sure you're on the latest version (App Store → Updates). If a crash persists, please email us with: device model, iOS version, what you were doing when it crashed, and a screenshot if possible.

How do I request a feature?

We'd love to hear it. Email dhfu94086@gmail.com with "Feature Request" in the subject line. Real-user requests drive our roadmap.

What to include in your email

To help us help you faster, please include the following when it applies:

  • Device model (e.g., iPhone 15 Pro)
  • iOS version (Settings → General → About → Software Version)
  • App version (Settings → About Kingly → Version)
  • What you were doing when the issue happened
  • What you expected to happen vs. what actually happened
  • Screenshots or screen recordings if possible
