FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does Kingly connect to my bank or brokerage?

No. Kingly is a journal, not a trading or aggregation tool. You log holdings, transactions, and balances manually. This keeps the app squarely outside regulated financial activity and your credentials never leave your device.

Where is my data stored?

All data is stored locally in the encrypted iOS app sandbox. There is no cloud backup of financial data, no remote server, and no account. If you delete the app, your data is deleted.

Can I export my data?

Yes. The Settings tab includes a full JSON export of every account, transaction, holding, budget, and theme preference. You can also share that file to any app that accepts it — iCloud Drive, Files, AirDrop, or email to yourself.

Is the app free?

Kingly is a one-time purchase with no subscriptions. All current features are included; future major updates may be released as separate paid upgrades.

Does the app require an internet connection?

No. Kingly works fully offline. An internet connection is never required to use any feature.
